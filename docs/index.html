Ghost Studio · for the team

Get Ghost Studio in one line

One-click ads with script, pictures, narrator and camera moves. It runs on your own computer and is free. No account and no download: copy one line, paste, press Enter. After that, just double-click the icon on your Desktop.

  1. Paste this line in Terminal

    Press ⌘ Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter. Paste the line with ⌘ Cmd + V and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vishnusunilwork-lgtm/ghost-studio/main/install/mac.sh | bash

    Paste this line in PowerShell

    Click Start, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste the line with a right-click (or Ctrl + V) and press Enter.

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vishnusunilwork-lgtm/ghost-studio/main/install/windows.ps1 | iex

  2. Wait about 5 minutes, once

    The first time, Ghost Studio installs itself and then opens in your browser. Keep the black window open while you use it.

After that

  • Open it: double-click the Ghost Studio app icon on your Desktop (Mac: also in Launchpad, drag it to your Dock).
  • Updates: when you see Update now at the top of the app, click it. It reloads by itself.
  • Problem? Click Copy problem report in the app and paste it to Vishnu on Slack.
  • Stop it: close the black window.
Your ads stay on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.
